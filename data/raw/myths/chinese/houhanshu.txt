<td style="width:50%;"><b>後漢書</b><br />(南朝劉宋) <a href="/wiki/Author:%E8%8C%83%E6%9B%84" title="Author:范曄">范曄</a>
<p>(西晉) <a href="/wiki/Author:%E5%8F%B8%E9%A6%AC%E5%BD%AA" title="Author:司馬彪">司馬彪</a> <small style="color:var(--color-destructive--visited,#996666)"><span style="color:transparent;font-size:0px">〈</span>（《<a href="/wiki/%E5%85%AB%E5%AE%B6%E5%BE%8C%E6%BC%A2%E6%9B%B8" title="八家後漢書">續漢書</a>》作者，有八〈志〉30卷補入范曄所著《後漢書》）<span style="color:transparent;font-size:0px">〉</span></small>
</p><p>註：(唐) 章懷太子 <a href="/wiki/Author:%E6%9D%8E%E8%B3%A2_(%E5%94%90)" title="Author:李賢 (唐)">李賢</a>
</p><p>(南朝蕭梁)<a href="/w/index.php?title=Author:%E5%8A%89%E6%98%AD&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Author:劉昭（页面不存在）">劉昭</a><small style="color:var(--color-destructive--visited,#996666)"><span style="color:transparent;font-size:0px">〈</span>《續漢志》原注者<span style="color:transparent;font-size:0px">〉</span></small>
</p>

<tbody><tr>
<td><span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;"><a class="mw-selflink selflink">後漢書</a></span>•一百二十卷<small style="color:var(--color-destructive--visited,#996666)"><span style="color:transparent;font-size:0px">〈</span>內府刊本<span style="color:transparent;font-size:0px">〉</span></small><span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">本紀</span>十卷、<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">列傳</span>八十卷，宋范蔚宗撰。唐章懷太子賢注。蔚宗事蹟具<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;"><a href="/wiki/%E5%AE%8B%E6%9B%B8" title="宋書">宋書</a></span>本傳。賢事蹟具<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">唐書</span>本傳。考<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;"><a href="/wiki/%E9%9A%8B%E6%9B%B8" title="隋書">隋志</a></span>載范<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">書</span>九十七卷，<a href="/wiki/%E6%96%B0%E5%94%90%E6%9B%B8" title="新唐書">新</a>、舊<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">唐書</span>則作九十二卷，互有不同。惟<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;"><a href="/wiki/%E5%AE%8B%E5%8F%B2" title="宋史">宋志</a></span>作九十卷，與今本合。然此書歷代相傳，無所亡佚。考<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;"><a href="/wiki/%E8%88%8A%E5%94%90%E6%9B%B8" title="舊唐書">舊唐志</a></span>又載章懷太子注<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">後漢書</span>一百卷。今本九十卷，中分子卷者凡十。是章懷作注之時，始並爲九十卷，以就成數。<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">唐志</span>析其子卷數之，故雲一百。<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">宋志</span>合其子卷數之，故仍九十。其實一也。又隋、唐<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">志</span>均別有蔚宗<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">後漢書論贊</span>五卷，<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">宋志</span>始不著録。疑唐以前<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">論贊</span>與本書別行，亦宋人散入書內。然<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;"><a href="/wiki/%E5%8F%B2%E9%80%9A" title="史通">史通</a>•論贊篇</span>曰：「馬遷自序傳後曆寫諸篇，各敘其意。既而班固變爲詩體，號之曰<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">述</span>。蔚宗改彼<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">述</span>名，呼之以<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">贊</span>。固之總述，合在一篇，使其條貫有序。
<p>蔚宗後書，乃各附本事，書於卷末，篇目相離，斷絕失序。夫每卷立論，其煩已多，而嗣論以贊，爲黷彌甚。亦猶文士制碑序終而續以銘曰，釋氏演法義盡而宣以偈言」云云。則唐代范書<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">論贊</span>已綴卷末矣。史志別出一目，所未詳也。范撰是書，以<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">志</span>屬謝瞻。范敗後，瞻悉蠟以覆車，遂無傳本。今本八<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">志</span>凡三十卷，別題梁剡令劉昭注。據陳振孫<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">書録解題</span>，乃宋乾興初判國子監孫奭建議校勘，以昭所注司馬彪<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;"><a href="/wiki/%E7%BA%8C%E6%BC%A2%E5%BF%97" class="mw-redirect" title="續漢志">續漢書志</a></span>與范<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">書</span>合爲一編。案<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">隋志</span>載司馬彪<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">續漢書</span>八十三卷，<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">唐書</span>亦同。<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">宋志</span>惟載劉昭<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">補注後漢志</span>三十卷，而彪書不著録。是至宋僅存其<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">志</span>，故移以補<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">後漢書</span>之闕。其不曰<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">續漢志</span>而曰<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">後漢志</span>是已併入范<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">書</span>之稱矣。
</p>
或謂酈道元<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;"><a href="/wiki/%E6%B0%B4%E7%B6%93%E6%B3%A8" title="水經注">水經注</a></span>嘗引司馬彪<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">州郡志</span>，疑其先已別行。又謂杜佑<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;"><a href="/wiki/%E9%80%9A%E5%85%B8" title="通典">通典</a></span>述科舉之制，以<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">後漢書</span>、<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">續漢志</span>連類而舉，疑唐以前已並八<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">志</span>入范<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">書</span>，似未確也。自八<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">志</span>合併之後，諸書徵引，但題<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">後漢書</span>某志。儒者或不知爲司馬彪書，故何焯<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">義門讀書記</span>曰：「八<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">志</span>，司馬紹統之作。（案紹統，彪之字也。）本漢末諸儒所傳，而述於晉初。劉昭注補，別有<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">總敘</span>。緣諸本或失載劉<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">敘</span>，故孫北海<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">藤陰劄記</span>亦誤出蔚宗志律曆之文」云云。考洪邁<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;"><a href="/wiki/%E5%AE%B9%E9%BD%8B%E9%9A%A8%E7%AD%86" title="容齋隨筆">容齋隨筆</a></span>，已誤以八<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">志</span>爲范<span style="text-decoration: underline;-moz-text-decoration-style: wavy;text-decoration-style: wavy;text-underline-offset:0.2em;">書</span>，則其誤不自孫承澤始。今於此三十卷並題司馬彪名，庶以祛流俗之訛焉。<ul id="plainSister" style="display:inline-block; font-size:93%; line-height:normal; list-style-type:none; <p>本作品原文沒有句讀（標點）。今人為方便閱讀而添加標點，其著作權問題可能涉及以下幾種情況：
</p>
<ol><li>維基文庫用戶自行為原文添加標點符號，其貢獻依據<a href="https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:CC_BY-SA_4.0%E5%8D%8F%E8%AE%AE%E6%96%87%E6%9C%AC" class="extiw" title="w:Wikipedia:CC BY-SA 4.0协议文本">知识共享 署名-相同方式共享 4.0协议（CC BY-SA 4.0）</a>及GNU自由文档许可证（GFDL）的条款释出。</li>
<li>1999年7月12日，中华人民共和国国家版权局版权管理司关于古籍标点等著作权问题的答复《<a href="/wiki/%E6%9D%83%E5%8F%B81999%E7%AC%AC45%E5%8F%B7" class="mw-redirect" title="权司1999第45号">权司1999第45号</a>》，认为仅加标点不足以有创作性，但按照《<a href="/wiki/%E4%B8%AD%E5%8D%8E%E4%BA%BA%E6%B0%91%E5%85%B1%E5%92%8C%E5%9B%BD%E6%B0%91%E6%B3%95%E9%80%9A%E5%88%99" title="中华人民共和国民法通则">中华人民共和国民法通则</a>》（现由《<a href="/wiki/%E4%B8%AD%E5%8D%8E%E4%BA%BA%E6%B0%91%E5%85%B1%E5%92%8C%E5%9B%BD%E6%B0%91%E6%B3%95%E5%85%B8" title="中华人民共和国民法典">中华人民共和国民法典</a>》取代其地位）的公平和等价有偿原则，利用他人的智力劳动，至少应当支付相应的对价。此处民法通则的公平和等价有偿原则与著作权是分别的话题。</li>
<li>中華民國94年（2005年）4月15日，中華民國經濟部智慧財產局<a rel="nofollow" class="external text" href="https://www1.tipo.gov.tw/ct.asp?ctnode=7448&amp;mp=1&amp;xitem=217120">智慧財產局解釋令函</a>（<a rel="nofollow" class="external text" href="https://web.archive.org/web/20190916043948/https://www.tipo.gov.tw/ct.asp?ctNode=7448&amp;mp=1&amp;xItem=217120">存档</a>）也認爲僅對古文加標點不足以取得新著作權。另請參見：章忠信《著作權筆記·<a rel="nofollow" class="external text" href="http://www.copyrightnote.org/ArticleContent.aspx?ID=2&amp;aid=251">句讀的著作權保護</a>》。</li>
<li>由人工智慧（AI）自動添加的標點符號，若僅屬機械性處理，且不包含足以構成原創性創作的人類智力成果，依照美國著作權法的原創性及人類作者原則，通常無法因此獨立取得著作權。其他國家或地區的法律規定可能有所不同，應依其適用法律判斷。參見：《<a rel="nofollow" class="external text" href="https://www.copyright.gov/ai/ai_policy_guidance.pdf">Copyright Registration Guidance: Works Containing Material Generated by Artificial Intelligence</a>》 與 《<a rel="nofollow" class="external text" href="https://www.copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-2-Copyrightability-Report.pdf">Copyright and Artificial Intelligence, Part 2: Copyrightability</a>》。</li></ol>
